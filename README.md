# Interactive Personal Finance Management Dashboard (Microsoft Excel)

## Project description
Developed a dynamic Personal Finance Dashboard in Microsoft Excel using daily financial data for the year 2024 to monitor income, expenses, savings, and financial balance on a monthly basis. The dashboard utilizes Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers to transform raw daily financial records into meaningful insights. It enables users to track spending patterns, evaluate savings, and analyze investment distribution such as Business, Securities, and Cryptocurrency for effective financial planning.

## Questions / KPIs 

- What is the total monthly income and expenses in 2024?
- How does the monthly balance vary throughout the year?
- What is the monthly savings amount after expenses?
- How are fixed and non-fixed costs contributing to total expenses?
- What is the income ratio for each month?
- How are investments distributed across Business, Securities, and Cryptocurrency?
- Are monthly financial goals being met based on income and expenses?
- Which month has the highest savings and lowest expenses?

## Process 

- Imported daily personal finance data for the year 2024 into Microsoft Excel.
- Cleaned and structured the dataset by organizing income, expenses, and cost-related fields.
- Performed monthly calculations such as savings, balance, and income ratio in the Processing sheet.
- Created Pivot Tables to summarize income, expenses, and savings on a monthly basis.
- Developed Pivot Charts to visualize monthly trends and investment distribution.
- Added Slicers and designed an interactive dashboard for dynamic financial analysis.

## Project Insights

- Monthly income and expenses show noticeable variations across different months in 2024.
- Fixed costs such as Mortgage and Credit Payments contribute significantly to total expenses.
- Savings fluctuate based on the difference between income and non-fixed expenditures.
- Investment distribution highlights allocation towards Business, Securities, and Cryptocurrency.
- Some months reflect a lower balance due to increased liabilities or financial commitments.
- Income ratio analysis helps in evaluating financial stability across months.

## Final Conclusion

The Personal Finance Dashboard developed using the 2024 financial dataset provides a comprehensive overview of monthly income, expenses, savings, and investment distribution. It transforms raw daily financial data into interactive visual insights that support efficient financial monitoring and budgeting. The dashboard enables users to analyze spending habits, manage liabilities, and track financial goals, thereby improving overall financial planning and decision-making.
